## Iteration 2 candidates

Deltas from the options are added to I1-imp. The candidates are estimates until each one is built as its own CAD variant. Unit cost is the first engine, hybrid build strategy, P50. Development cost is the bottom-up programme cost plus the options' development cost.

| Candidate | Options | Mass (kg) | T/W | Isp SL (s) | pc (bar) | LOX / CH4 pump (bar) | T1 ($M) | Development ($B) | Lowest TRL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I2-A: Integrate and weld (low risk) | K1, I1, I3, I2, I4, N2, C1, N5 | 1749 | 160 | 333.7 | 350 | 660 / 815 | 3.31 | 0.46 | 4 |
| I2-B: I2-A plus 10 bar pump inlet | K1, I1, I3, I2, I4, N2, C1, N5, Y1, C2 | 1592 | 176 | 333.7 | 350 | 642 / 805 | 3.20 | 0.47 | 4 |
| I2-C: I2-B uprated to 400 bar | K1, I1, I3, I2, I4, N2, C1, N5, C2, Y2 | 1570 | 178 | 336.2 | 400 | 737 / 955 | 3.90 | 0.51 | 4 |
| Raptor 3 (public) | | 1525 | 184 | | ~350 | | | | |

- I2-A: Everything here is TRL 4 or higher and costs little to develop. K1 and C1 are corrections the physics asks for anyway: the fuel-rich preburner has almost no kinetic margin at 4 ms, and the CH4 bearings already run at 2.3 million DN.
- I2-B: Faster, smaller turbopumps. The CH4 shaft then reaches 3.1 million DN, beyond hybrid bearings, so hydrostatic bearings (C2) come with it. The inlet pressure is a vehicle requirement (tank pressure), not an engine part.
- I2-C: Highest thrust-to-weight. Needs the 900 K ox-rich hot section (C4, TRL 4) and SX fuel-turbine blades and two-stage turbines; Y2 already includes those and the 10 bar inlet.
